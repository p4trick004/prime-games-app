Frequently asked questions

        

          

            Do I need to pay a deposit?
            
No deposit at all. We verify your NIDA ID, driver's licence or passport at delivery, plus one emergency contact — that's our only security check.

          

          

            Which areas do you deliver to?
            
All of Dar es Salaam, within 1–2 hours. Listed areas have fixed round-trip fees; if your area is not listed we agree the fee with the rider when confirming.

          

          

            How does the Night Package work?
            
We deliver at 8:00 PM and collect at 10:00 AM the next morning — TZS 15,000 per night with the full Starter specs: console, 2 controllers and up to 5 games.

          

          

            Can I pay on delivery?
            
Yes — cash or M-Pesa directly to the rider when the console arrives. You can also pay by M-Pesa after we confirm your booking.

          

          

            What if the PS5 or a controller gets damaged?
            
Normal wear is fine. Real damage is charged at repair cost, and water damage or theft at replacement value — which is why we keep an ID and emergency contact on file.

          

          

            Can I extend my rental?
            
Yes — message us on WhatsApp before your rental ends and pay the extra days. Multi-day discounts still apply to the extended period.
